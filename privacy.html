🌿 Pruner Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

Pruner is a Chrome extension that helps you unfollow Instagram accounts that don't follow you back. We take your privacy seriously.

Data We Collect

Pruner does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data stays on your device.

What Pruner Stores Locally

Pruner stores the following data locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API:

  • Your list of non-followers (derived from files you upload)
  • The list of accounts you have unfollowed
  • Your session progress and stats

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Instagram Data

Pruner uses your Instagram session cookies solely to make unfollow requests on your behalf. Pruner never stores, reads, or transmits your Instagram credentials or password.

Third Party Services

Pruner does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
